04 / POINT OF VIEW

AF—01

Read the system.
Ask better questions.

Instrumentation draws me to the part of an industrial process that is easy to miss: how we measure what is happening, decide what to do next, and improve precision without wasting resources.

I’m studying Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering at FISAT and learning through project teams, student roles and independent work. That curiosity extends to industrial automation and OT security, and to embedded hardware, IoT and robotics. I’m exploring these fields, not presenting myself as an expert in each.

Writing, publication design and speaking sit alongside engineering for a reason: technical work also needs to be understood by the people who use it. I like moving between building, investigating and explaining, while being clear about what I have done and what I am still learning.
